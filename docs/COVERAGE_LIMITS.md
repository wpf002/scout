# What Scout cannot see

Shown during onboarding. Plain language on purpose.

Scout only uses data it is allowed to have: public feeds, published records,
licensed data with a contract, and data you own and enrolled. That means the
picture is always partial, and the interface says so instead of hiding it.

**Sparse.** Most things are not in any lawful source. A person with no public
records, a vessel with its transponder off, an aircraft outside receiver
coverage: Scout shows nothing, and nothing is not evidence of anything.

**Lagged.** Every source has a delay. A satellite pass is days apart. A public
record is filed weeks after the event. The entity view shows the observation
time per source so you can see how old what you are looking at is.

**Partial by source.** Each entity view lists which sources were checked, which
returned data, which returned nothing, and which you were not authorized to
use. A source that returned nothing is listed, not dropped.

**Uncertain.** Every link between two records carries a score and the evidence
behind it, one click away. A score is never shown on its own. Some pairs are
marked indeterminate and stay that way until a person decides.

**Not surveillance.** Scout will not intercept communications, reach into
systems it is not enrolled for, search the internet for a face, or act on its
own. If you need one of those, Scout is the wrong tool and will tell you so.
